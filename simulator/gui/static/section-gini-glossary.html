Terminology

Term Definition
Grouping (S) A partition of the events under consideration into terminal groups.
Leaf (ℓ) A current uncut group.
Cut (γ) A partition of one leaf into two or more children C(γ).
fitness(leaf) (v(ℓ)) The leaf scored as packed at size s, with no further cut.
OPT(leaf) (OPT(ℓ)) The best score obtainable from the leaf if further cuts remain allowed under the remaining evaluation budget.
Container size (s) Event count of one packed unit; the common packing budget.
Evaluation budget Compute and wall-clock allowed for enumerating and scoring cuts.
Unit (i) One equal-size container after the grouping is packed at size s.
Population weight (wi) Unit i's share of the parent population, ni/n.
Group-wide rate (pg,i) One selection rate for unit i, averaged across training queries.
Query-conditional rate (pi,q) Fraction of unit i that query q selected.
Poisson parameter (λ) Expected selected-event count in the unit being modeled.
Group-wide skip ratio (Vmarg(S,s)) Estimated skip ratio from the group-wide rates.
Query-conditional skip ratio (V(S,s)) Estimated skip ratio that keeps the query index.
Weighted Gini (G(S)) Inequality of the group-wide rate across units.
Extra groups (Δk) How many groups the cut adds.
Incremental gain Fitness change per extra group, Δv/Δk.
