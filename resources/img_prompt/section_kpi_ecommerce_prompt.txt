LAYOUT
The map is a recursive subdivision, five levels deep. Level 1 is the
whole map. Level 2 is the department. Levels 3 to 5 are product lines.
Maximum split depth is therefore 4.

Level 1 cuts into 16 departments. That count is derived, not chosen.
With a 0.75 continue probability and three levels below the
department, expected leaves per department would be L(3) = 4.5625,
where L(0) = 1 and L(r) = 0.25 + 1.5*L(r-1). The guaranteed-depth pass
below raises that to 5.152 measured over the generator. A target of 80
leaves gives 80 / 5.152 = 15.53, so 16 departments, for 82.4 expected
leaves.

Below level 2 the depth is built like a skip list: a cell continues to
the next level with probability 0.75 and otherwise stops, so depth is
geometric rather than uniform and departments differ in how finely
they are cut.

A scan-and-mutate pass then repairs the runs that stopped too early. A
department whose first roll fails holds one rectangle filling its own
cell, which draws as a titled empty box with no substructure at all.
So after the probabilistic pass, every department is scanned: one that
has not reached level 4 is cut again, deepest cell first, until it
does. Every department therefore contains level 3 and level 4. The
repair runs after the fact rather than biasing the 0.75 rule that
produced the run, and it is refused only when the minimum leaf size
makes the depth impossible, which rejects the whole composition.

Every cut splits the longer axis, at a fraction between about a third
and two thirds, so cells stay near-square and no cut lands near an
edge. A cut is refused when either side would fall below the minimum
leaf size, and that minimum is set by the label: a leaf must hold its
subcategory name at a legible size, wrapped to two lines where it must
be. Keep the minimum near-square for the same reason the cuts are — an
elongated floor makes elongated leaves, whatever the cuts above it
did. The cold pocket is reserved as a corner block, never an edge
strip: a strip holding a fifth of a 16:9 map is many times longer than
it is tall.

Every rectangle is labelled. Each department carries its name at level
2 in a title cell of its own — a light orange ground inside a visible
1px orange border, spanning the department's width — and every leaf
carries its subcategory name, set as large as the cell allows and
wrapped where it must be. No demand figure and no size is printed
anywhere on the map; the rate is read from the legend axis. Leaves
carry no card ground, so the map reads as one surface.

The legend at the foot is the colour axis and nothing else. Fill is
the only channel the map uses, so there is nothing else to decode: no
area note and no band key. It spans the width of the map, titled
"Selection rate", running from 0% at the green end to 25% at the red
end, ticked every 5%, with the line "Query demand % with 2 weeks of
ingestion" beneath it.
