OUTPUT
One static PNG, 1920 x 1080, warm off-white ground, single light palette.
Accepted path: resources/img/section_c_go_live_process.png.

SUBJECT
The six-step engineering process on Go Live on Databricks Lakehouse.
The figure teaches one thing: the left-pane sections are one directed
chain of actions. It is the illustration of the page opening and of
every left-pane section number. It is not a scheduler, a recommendation,
or a scored layout.

STYLE
Editorial flat 2D process diagram. A #F3F3F3 header band, 130px tall and
closed by a 1px rule, carries the title block. No perspective, realism,
3D, shadows, gradients, UI chrome, dashboard widgets, server racks, or
emojis. System-ui labels; ui-monospace for every identifier. Rounded
corners about 10px on action rectangles. 1px rules. Single-stroke
directed arrows with an open arrowhead.

COLOURS
Use the same explainer family as Sections A, D, and E:
#FCFCFB canvas . #7A1F2B wine-red . #F8EEF0 warm-rose .
#1B4D3E dark-green . #EEF4F0 cool-sage . #0B0B0B ink .
#52514E secondary . #DEDEDE rules . #F3F3F3 header band .
#FFFFFF card ground.
Wine-red stroke on every action rectangle and every arrow. Warm-rose
fill on the small sequence circles only. Action bodies stay white.
Do not use orange. Colour is never the only state channel: every
circle also prints its number in ink.

TITLE
Visible title, 35 Bold ink, left-aligned in the header band:
  The go-live process
One subtitle, 17.5 secondary, under the title:
  Six actions. Numbers match the left-pane sections.

THE CHAIN (fixed -- do not vary)
Six actions, in this order, each the exact left-pane section title:

  1  Collect a target sample
  2  Identify split decision trees
  3  Estimate leaf skipping potential
  4  Inspect the holdout
  5  Implement data ingestion
  6  Implement query optimization

No other action appears. No sub-step is drawn inside a rectangle.

COMPOSITION
Six rounded rectangles in one vertical chain, centred on the canvas
below the header band. Equal width, equal height, equal gap. A directed
arrow joins each rectangle to the next: 1 to 2, 2 to 3, 3 to 4, 4 to 5,
5 to 6. No side branch, no back edge, no join.

Each rectangle carries:

- a small circle at its top-left corner, sitting on the rectangle's
  stroke so the circle is the first mark a reader meets;
- the sequence number 1 through 6 inside that circle, 15 mono Semibold
  ink, centred;
- the action title on one line, 18 system-ui Semibold ink, centred in
  the remaining width.

The circle is the only ornament. No icon, badge, chevron, or status
fill on the rectangle body.

Steps 1 to 4 are the design-and-estimate stretch. Steps 5 and 6 are
the Lakehouse write and read. That grouping is not labelled on the
figure; the numbers already order the work.

NO LEGEND AND NO CAPTION
The last arrow is the last mark. Every action is labelled where it
sits. No caption line, no footer, no "start" or "end" chip.

TYPE SCALE (base sizes at 1920 x 1080)
title 35 Bold . subtitle 17.5 . action 18 Semibold .
sequence number 15 mono Semibold.

KEEP OUT
- No seventh action and no collapsed "and so on".
- No action text other than the six titles above.
- No swimlane, role, or system column.
- No clock, cost, or duration on a step.
- No winning path, recommended step, or skipped step.
- No Databricks logo, cluster diagram, or server rack.
- No table, formula, or sample row.
- No caption line.
- No distinction carried by colour alone.

PRODUCTION
Draw at 3840 x 2160 and downsample once to 1920 x 1080 with LANCZOS.
Load SFNS.ttf and SFNSMono.ttf from /System/Library/Fonts; select
weight with set_variation_by_name and cache by (size, weight, mono).
Use anchor= for text. Draw arrowheads as line glyphs, not Unicode.

All six rectangles share width, height, corner radius, and stroke.
Sequence circles share diameter. Gaps between rectangles are equal.
Labels must clear arrow shafts by at least 20 source pixels. Fail the
render on clipping, overlap, a missing number, a number that does not
match the title beside it, a second arrow from any rectangle, or a
title that wraps to a second line. After rendering, read the PNG back
and inspect it visually before acceptance. The PNG at the accepted
path is rendered and accepted from this specification; the renderer
that produced it is deleted with acceptance. A later visual change
starts a new authoring pass.
