SUBJECT
A Finviz-style usage map of social content. Nested rectangles group
posts by format. Tile area is impressions. Fill brightness is
engagement rate. The dim group is content that drew none. It is the
illustration of Business Signal in Cold Data §2.4. It is not an
allocator and not a recommendation.

STYLE
Editorial flat 2D squarified treemap, one legend strip under the map.
No perspective, realism, 3D, shadows, gradients, UI chrome, dashboard
widgets, server racks, or emojis. System-ui labels; ui-monospace for
every identifier and number. 1px group rules. Tile corners about 2px.

COLOURS
House explainer family only. No third hue.
#FCFCFB canvas (paper, coldest fill).
#EEF4F0 sage (warm-cold fill).
#1B4D3E dark-green (hottest fill).
#7A1F2B wine (cold-tile stroke only).
#F8EEF0 rose (unused except as a hatch field if the hatch needs a tint).
#0B0B0B ink. #52514E secondary. #DEDEDE rules. #FFFFFF card ground.

Brightness is the KPI channel: higher engagement is deeper green, lower
engagement fades toward paper. Colour is never the only state channel.
Every cold tile also has a 2px wine stroke, a 45-degree hatch, and its
printed engagement rate. Warm and hot tiles have no hatch.
