OUTPUT
One looping APNG, 1920 x 1080, warm off-white ground, single light palette.

SUBJECT
One abstracted evaluation of a data split. The figure presents / introduces the cycle a
search repeats at a leaf: list candidate cuts, score each cut, replace the
leaf with the children of the illustrated cut. It is a mechanism diagram,
not a solver, not a layout score, and not a recommendation.

STYLE
Editorial flat 2D diagram, one tree in the centre, a three-phase process
rail across the top. No perspective, realism, 3D, shadows, gradients, UI
chrome, dashboard widgets, server racks, or emojis. System-ui labels;
ui-monospace for every identifier and number. Rounded corners about 8px
on cards and 6px on tokens. 1px rules.

COLOURS
Use the same explainer family as the Problem Statement illustration and the Gini example:
#FCFCFB canvas . #7A1F2B wine-red . #F8EEF0 warm-rose .
#1B4D3E dark-green . #EEF4F0 cool-sage . #0B0B0B ink .
#52514E secondary . #DEDEDE rules . #F3F3F3 header band .
#FFFFFF card ground.
Do not use orange. Colour is never the only state channel.

Fitness numbers are authored demonstration values, not a live score and
not tied to the auto-parts Gini example. Child event counts must sum to
parent.events. The illustrated cut is g1 so the animation has a story;
it is never labelled best, winner, or recommended.

TITLE
None. The raster carries no title line and no subtitle. The figure is
published under a section heading -- "Recursive Partitioning with a
Decision Tree" -- and repeating that inside the image would print the
title twice on the page. The rail is the first element on the canvas.

PROCESS RAIL
A horizontal rail under the title, three equal stations, always visible:
  [1] Enumerate
  [2] Evaluate
  [3] Split
Wine rounded badge (24 x 24, white mono 14) on each station. The active
phase uses a wine top rule + warm-rose fill + ink station title. Idle
phases use white fill, no top rule, and secondary type. No station prints
the word "now"; the phase is carried by the fill, by whether the top rule
is drawn at all, and by the weight of the title -- never by wine alone.
A single-stroke arrow joins adjacent stations. The rail does not slide;
only the active station changes.

Each station carries the description of what its phase does, set directly
beneath the tab and flush with its left edge, wrapped to the tab's width.
All three descriptions are on the page in every frame; the active one is
ink and the other two are greyed. Nothing describing the operation is ever
withheld -- the reader can see what all three phases do before the loop
reaches them. There is no separate sentence under the tree.

COMPONENT -- THE TREE (centre, shared geometry across frames)
Circular root node labelled parent.label -- "R0 Input" -- with the
derived "size = 1,200" under the name. The input node is constant: it
never fades, dims, empties, or vacates its slot at any point in the loop.
A predicate's two children are named for the answer to the condition,
"Yes" and "No", not for the side of the threshold. Below it, three candidate cards in
a fixed row, one per candidates[] entry, equal width, always in dictionary
order.

Each card shows:
  condition row  one line carrying both the split and its score. On the
                 left, the condition derived from the entry: a multi-way
                 grain reads "Group by [<column>]"; a local predicate reads
                 "[<column>] <op> <value>". On the right, flush with the
                 card's right edge, the score as "Fitness = 0.41". Both in
                 mono Semibold at the same size, so the row reads across as
                 two peers rather than a label and a caption. The score is
                 greyed until Evaluate; the condition never greys.
  children       small rectangles named by id, each printing its size
                 as "size = …"

Grain and predicate are not distinguished. There is no kind token and no
second colour: every card is drawn in the same white ground, the same
#DEDEDE outline, and the same ink type, so the reader compares conditions
rather than categories.

Every count in the figure -- root and children alike -- is written
"size = <n>". Do not overlay numbers on a shape that already carries a
name; the id sits on its own line above the size.

ANIMATION EFFECT
A forward loop of three rest poses and three short transitions. No
reverse playback, no bounce, no easing that overshoots. Title and subtitle
stay still for the whole loop. The three candidate columns stay
registered: layout does not jump; only state changes.

Nothing in this figure appears, disappears, or moves. Every component --
the input node, all three cards, every fitness score, and the children of
the illustrated cut with the arrows that reach them -- is drawn in its
final position in all six frames. A component that is not yet part of the
story is greyed out, and a phase restores it to full ink; that is the only
way anything changes state. The reader therefore sees the whole shape of
the operation from the first frame and watches it fill in, rather than
watching pieces arrive. Grey-out is #B6B5B2 for type and #DEDEDE for
outlines and arrows, over the white card ground.

Total cycle 5.6 s -- the sum of the six durations below -- then it repeats
from phase 1. Restoring something from grey is given longer than greying it
out again: the reader needs to notice a component arriving, and does not
need to watch it leave.

1. ENUMERATE -- rest 1400 ms
   Station 1 is the active phase. R0 Input is solid, as it is throughout.
   The three candidate cards are fully drawn. Every fitness score is
   printed but greyed out, and so are the children of the illustrated cut
   and their arrows. Ink sentence under the tree:
     "List the cuts this leaf still allows."
   No card is emphasized.

2. TRANSITION A -- 400 ms  (Enumerate -> Evaluate)
   Fitness labels and values restore from grey to ink on all three cards
   together, same baseline, same weight. They do not count up, slide,
   appear, or arrive one after another -- they were already on the page. The active phase hands over from station 1 to station 2;
   the badges stay. The sentence under the tree cross-fades to the
   Evaluate sentence.
   One frame carries the whole transition, so both effects resolve to a
   still: the two stations each hold the fill and the top rule at half
   strength, which is what a handover looks like stopped; and the sentence
   is the incoming one at partial opacity. Two sentences drawn over each
   other on one baseline would be unreadable, so a cross-fade is never
   encoded that way.

3. EVALUATE -- rest 1600 ms
   Station 2 is the active phase. Every candidate card shows its derived
   fitness at full ink, labelled "fitness". Ink sentence:
     "Score every listed cut. No mark is a choice."
   Do not sort, dim, crown, star, or bold the highest fitness. All three
   cards keep equal weight.

4. TRANSITION B -- 500 ms  (Evaluate -> Split)
   The illustrated_cut (g1) stays at full ink. The other two cards grey
   out to dashed #DEDEDE outlines and secondary type; they stay in place
   so the list is not rewritten. The three arrows below the g1 card and
   the children R1, R2, R3 they point to restore from grey to wine; they
   do not grow, slide, or appear. R0 Input is untouched. Each child sits
   directly under its own listing in the card, separated from it by more
   than the child's own width so the pairing reads without the two
   colliding. The active phase hands over to station 3. The sentence
   cross-fades to the Split sentence.

5. SPLIT -- rest 1600 ms
   Station 3 is the active phase. R0 Input is unchanged. R1, R2, R3 stand
   at full ink as the new leaves, sizes derived. The two candidates that
   were not illustrated stay greyed but fully legible.
   Ink sentence:
     "Apply one illustrated cut. The children become the next leaves."
   Do not print "accepted", "best", "winner", or a rank.

6. TRANSITION C -- 100 ms  (Split -> Enumerate, start of next loop)
   The children and their arrows grey out again where they stand. The two
   receded cards return to full ink. Fitness greys out together. R0 Input
   is untouched, as always. The active phase returns to station 1. This is
   a reset, not a reverse of Transition B: nothing plays backward, and
   nothing moves in either direction.

Redraw only the bounding box of what changed on transition frames.
Do not blur the whole canvas. Encode the six steps as APNG frames in
this order; hold the rest poses for the times above; give each
transition its duration as the frame delay. Loop Frame 1 after
Transition C. No extra interstitial frame.

The raster carries no legend and no caption line. With grain and
predicate drawn alike there is no encoding left to name, and the three
ink sentences under the tree already say what each phase does.

TYPE SCALE (base sizes at 1920 x 1080)
Every size below is a base size. The renderer adds a +1pt bump to all of
them, once, inside the font loader and at no call site, so the scale stays
readable as one set of relative sizes.

counts mono 15 . condition row 15 mono Semibold, condition and
"Fitness = <v>" alike . tab descriptions 13.5.

KEEP OUT
- No "best", "optimal", "recommended", "winner", crown, medal, or star.
- No assignment of events to physical containers.
- No Gini, Lorenz, skip-ratio formula, Poisson symbol, or auto-parts
  brand names -- those belong to the continued example below this figure.
- No production constant, cooling schedule, beam width, or code.
- No distinction carried by colour alone.
- No second tree, no search-history trail, no evaluation-budget meter
  that implies a remaining-time recommendation.
- No particle burst, bounce, elastic overshoot, or 3D flip.

PRODUCTION
Small type and hairlines stay clean at final size. Frames run in
dictionary order, looping, with the hold and transition times above.
Arrowheads are drawn as line glyphs, not Unicode.

All geometry is generated from the instance dictionary. The three
candidate columns stay registered across frames so only state, not
layout, changes. Labels must clear arrow shafts by at least 20 source
pixels. Fail the render on clipping, overlap, a child whose events do
not sum to the parent, or a printed fitness that is not in the
dictionary. Every frame is inspected visually before acceptance.
